# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the product owner. The product may expand to additional users in the future, but it is currently designed around this context.

## Product Purpose

Home App is currently a cooking and nutrition app for managing recipes and their nutritional information. It exists to bring recipe management and nutrition management together in one tool. Success criteria beyond serving the current user is not yet defined.

## Positioning

Home App integrates recipe and nutrition workflows well in one product. This addresses a gap where other tools tend to be weaker on either the recipe side or the nutrition side.

## Operating Context

The app is self-hosted and used by a household for cooking and nutrition-related tasks. It is currently operated in English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current product focus: recipes and nutrition.
- The existing application includes ingredient search, nutrition details, serving and unit conversions, ingredient variants, and ingredient create/edit/delete workflows.
- Recipes are represented in the navigation but are currently under construction.
- The product may expand to other home-organization tasks and additional users later; the future scope is intentionally undecided.
- The app is self-hosted.
- The product should remain in English for now.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains the current web application, API contract, backend persistence, and frontend implementation.
- The frontend currently provides an Ingredients area and an under-construction Recipes area.
- No external testimonials, benchmarks, or other product evidence were provided.

## Product Principles

- Keep recipe and nutrition management integrated in one product.
- Prioritize practical usefulness for household cooking.
- Preserve self-hosted operation.
- Keep current product decisions focused while leaving room for broader home organization later.
